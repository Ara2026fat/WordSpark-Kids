About WordSpark Kids

🚀 Getting Started

🌟 Type your child's name and pick Spark's color.

🏝️ Tap any island on the map to start learning words and letters.

🍂 Once islands feel comfortable, the Seasons open — turning words into real sentences.

🚀 Then the Solar System unlocks — one deeper topic per planet.

🎮 Inside Every World

🎧 Every word comes with sound — tap it anytime to hear it again.

🧩 Each world ends with its own game — matching, sounds, drag & drop, or riddles.

👨‍👩‍👧 For You, the Parent

📊 Open Parent Dashboard anytime for real progress — not guesses.

🚗 The Recap Adventure re-tests old words to show what's truly remembered.

💬 Share a weekly report on WhatsApp with one tap.

🕌 Our Values Commitment

Every symbol, word, and story is checked so nothing shown to your child conflicts with Islamic values — no exceptions, reviewed continually as the app grows.
